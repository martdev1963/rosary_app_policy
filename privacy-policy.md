# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** March 12, 2025

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in our Catholic prayer and rosary app ("the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

**App identifier (Android):** `com.martdev1963.rosaryapp`  
**App identifier (iOS):** As published in the App Store

---

## Information We Collect

The App is designed with privacy in mind. We minimize data collection:

### 1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following information is stored only on your device and is **not** transmitted to our servers or any third party:

| Data | Purpose | Where stored |
|------|---------|--------------|
| Playback preferences | Remember your music volume and selected background track | Device storage (AsyncStorage / localStorage) |
| Reminder settings | Remember whether feast reminders are on/off and how many days in advance | Device storage |
| Subscription status | Know if you have an active premium subscription | Handled by your device's app store (Google Play / App Store) |

### 2. Subscription and Payment Information

If you subscribe to our premium features (monthly or yearly plan):

- **Payment processing** is handled entirely by **Google Play** (Android) or the **App Store** (iOS).
- We do **not** collect, store, or have access to your payment details, credit card information, or billing address.
- We only receive confirmation from the store that your subscription is active. This verification happens on your device; we do not maintain our own servers with your subscription status.

Please refer to [Google Play's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) for how the respective stores handle your payment data.

### 3. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do **not** collect:

- Name, email address, or other contact information
- Account credentials (the App does not require you to create an account)
- Location data
- Device identifiers for advertising
- Usage analytics or behavioral tracking
- Cookies for tracking (on web, we may use essential storage only for app preferences)

---

## How We Use Your Information

- **Local preferences** (volume, music track, reminders): Used solely to provide and personalize your experience within the App. This data never leaves your device.
- **Subscription status**: Used to determine which features (e.g., full Psalm library, text-to-speech) you can access. Verification is performed on-device via the app store APIs.

---

## Data Storage and Security

- All preference data is stored locally on your device.
- We do not operate servers that collect or store your personal data.
- If you uninstall the App, all locally stored data is removed with it.
